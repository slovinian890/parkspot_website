Our story

Why we started in Dubrovnik

Historic centers like Dubrovnik's Old Town were built for walking, not cars — which makes parking one of the most stressful parts of any visit.

ParkSpot turns that scarcity into opportunity. Locals with an unused driveway or garage can list it in minutes. Visitors can book a guaranteed spot before they even arrive — and skip circling the city walls looking for one.
