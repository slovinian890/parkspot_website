How it works

One app. Both sides of the gate.

Whether you need a space or have one going to waste, it takes about two minutes to get going.

01

Search where you're headed

Drop a pin on your destination and see every private space around it, with the real hourly price up front. Filter for covered, EV charging, CCTV or a bay big enough for a van.

02

Book it before you leave

An hour, a day, or every weekday this month. Instant-book spaces are confirmed on the spot, so the space is legally yours when you arrive.

03

Drive in and get on with it

Turn-by-turn to the entrance, then a QR code or gate code to get in. A timestamped photo at check-in protects you if anything's disputed later.

01

List the space in minutes

Pin the entrance, add a few photos and say when it's free. If you're out from 8 to 5, that's eight billable hours a day you weren't using.

02

Price it with real demand

We show you what nearby spaces charge and when your street gets busy — match days, market mornings, the evening theatre rush — so you're not guessing.

03

Approve, or let it run itself

Accept requests one by one, or switch on instant-book and sync your calendar so the space blocks itself when you need the car at home. Payouts land weekly.

What's in the app

Built around what actually goes wrong when you park.

Every feature here exists because something about parking is annoying. None of it is decoration.

A map of spaces you can't see from the street

Private driveways, residents' garages and small courtyard lots — the ones behind gates. Prices are per hour and shown before you tap, so there's nothing to work out at a barrier.

Get in without meeting anyone

A QR code or gate code arrives with your booking. No waiting around for someone to hand over a key or a fob.

Running late? Extend from the car

One tap adds time if the space is still free after you. No sprinting back to feed a meter.

Photos settle arguments

Both sides photograph the bay at check-in and check-out. Timestamped, stored, and the only thing support needs to look at.

Everyone is a verified name

ID and licence checks on both sides before a first booking. You know who's parking on your drive, and whose drive you're on.

Message the host, split the bill

Ask which gate, tell them you're ten minutes out, or send one link to split a long stay with whoever's in the car. It all stays attached to the booking.

Questions

The things people ask before their first booking.

Still unsure about something? Write to us at hello@parkspot.app and a person will answer.

A garage sells you entry and hopes there's room. ParkSpot sells you one specific bay that nobody else can take, usually closer to where you're going and typically cheaper per hour. You also know the exact price before you drive, rather than reading a tariff board at a barrier.

Report it in the app and you're refunded in full, immediately. We'll also find and book you the nearest available alternative and cover the difference in price. Hosts who repeatedly let this happen are removed.

Every host is ID-verified before a space goes live, and you can see their history and reviews. Check-in and check-out photos are timestamped on both sides, so the condition your car arrived in is on record. Many listings are behind a locked gate, which is more than a street space offers.

The host sets the hourly rate, typically between €1.20 and €2.50 in central Ljubljana. The price you see is the price you pay — no booking fee bolted on at checkout, no surge pricing when it rains. ParkSpot takes a service fee from the host's side.

Free cancellation up to an hour before your booking starts. Inside that hour the host keeps the first hour's rate, because they've turned other people away to hold it for you.

Keep the space clear and accessible during the hours you've marked as available. That's it. You're never required to be home, meet anyone, or hand over keys — access is a code or a QR scan. You can block dates whenever you like.
